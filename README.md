🔭 Current Research Direction

My current research exploration focuses on the intersection of AI and intelligent mobile/connected systems.

1. On-Device AI

Exploring machine learning systems that can perform inference closer to the device, with an emphasis on:

- Privacy
- Latency
- Energy efficiency
- Personalization
- Real-time inference

2. Mobile & Wearable Sensing

Investigating how signals from smartphones, wearables, and connected devices can be used to infer:

- Human activity
- Orientation and movement
- Context
- Interaction patterns
- Environmental state

3. Intelligent Human–Computer Interaction

Exploring how AI can make interfaces more adaptive by understanding user context, device state, and multimodal signals.

4. Intelligent IoT Systems

Investigating how distributed sensors and connected devices can cooperate with AI models to create more context-aware and predictive systems.

Sensing → Representation → Intelligence → Decision → Interaction

This perspective motivates my exploration of AI systems that operate on mobile and edge devices and interact naturally with people and connected environments.

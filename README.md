# Md. Rakibul Islam

### Research-oriented Software Engineer | AI/ML | Mobile & Edge Intelligence

I am a Senior Software Engineer at **Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh Ltd.** with a background in Computer Science and Engineering and a research focus on **intelligent mobile and edge systems**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **on-device AI, mobile and wearable sensing, computational photography, human–computer interaction, and IoT/edge intelligence**. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from East West University with a **CGPA of 3.94/4.00**, where I completed research on **Bengali speech recognition using recurrent neural networks**.

My broader research goal is to investigate how sensing, machine intelligence, and mobile/edge computing can work together to create **adaptive, context-aware, and human-centered intelligent systems**.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus

### Intelligent Mobile & Edge Systems

My primary research direction is the development of intelligent systems that combine **sensing, machine learning, mobile computing, and human interaction**.

**Research themes:**

- **On-device AI** — efficient, privacy-aware, low-latency machine learning
- **Mobile & Wearable Sensing** — activity, orientation, movement, and context inference
- **Intelligent HCI** — context-aware and multimodal human–computer interaction
- **Computational Photography** — AI-assisted imaging and intelligent camera systems
- **IoT & Edge Intelligence** — connected, distributed, and sensor-driven intelligence

### Research Perspective

**Sensing → Representation → Intelligence → Decision → Interaction**

I am particularly interested in moving beyond isolated AI models toward complete intelligent systems that understand their environment, infer context, make decisions, and interact naturally with people and devices.
